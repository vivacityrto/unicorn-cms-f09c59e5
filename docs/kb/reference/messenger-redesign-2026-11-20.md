# Messenger redesign (client bubble, Inbox, staff messaging, Help Center retirement)

Status as of 20 November 2026: **phase 1 (foundations) in review; the conversation access migration is applied to production, as is the broadcast recipient migration (PR 1604, whose edge function change deploys on merge).** Approved by Carl on 20 November 2026 with these decisions: tighten conversation access first; tickets appear as threads through an adapter, not merged into the conversation tables; schema-backed features (replies, edit and delete, reactions, pin and mute, search) come last; Help Center is removed as code only and its tables are kept until a later approved cleanup.

## Why

Four separate chat implementations existed (client Inbox, staff Communications, the Manage Client Messages tab, Help Center's CSC tab), each with its own composer, bubbles and send logic. The Ask Viv bubble and Help Center were two different bots. Clients had no single place to talk to Viv, their consultant and support.

## Shape

One kit, `src/components/messenger/`, fed by thread sources:

- **Contract** (`types.ts`): `MessengerThread`, `MessengerMessage`, `MessengerPerson` and `ThreadSource`. A source turns its own tables into these shapes; the components never know which table a thread came from.
- **Components:** `Messenger` (list plus conversation; `page` mode is two panes from the xl breakpoint, `dock` mode is one pane at a time for the bubble), `ThreadList`, `ConversationPane`, `MessageTimeline`, `Composer`.
- **Logic** (`messengerLogic.ts`, pure and tested): grouping, day dividers, "New messages", Seen from participants' read positions, typing label, filter and search, drafts.
- **Thread types:** Ask Viv, Your CSC, Support (a ticket is a thread with a status chip), From Vivacity (broadcasts, read only), Package, Task, Rock, General, Direct.
- **Rules kept:** client-written text is plain text (links only for http and https); drafts survive switching threads; Ask Viv replies only ever on a user action (no automatic AI).
- **No new dependency in phase 1.** Long threads show the newest 60 messages with "Show earlier messages". Virtualisation (react-virtuoso) can be added later if threads grow; verify its licence and status on npm first.

## Phases

1. **Foundations (this PR):** conversation access migration (applied) and the kit with tests.
2. **Client Inbox rebuilt on the kit (in review).** The Messages tab is the kit, fed by `adapters/tenantConversations.ts`; data hooks (`useClientCommunications`) are unchanged apart from carrying each conversation's read position. New in the tab: a "Sending…" bubble while a message goes, a "New messages" divider and opening at it, Seen from other participants' read positions (refreshed every 20 seconds), drafts per conversation, and attachments refreshed after upload. The All and Notifications tabs, the New message dialog, deep links (`?thread=`, `?conversation=`) and broadcast replies are unchanged. Typing indicators wait for phase 3, when both sides of a conversation use the kit.
3. **The bubble as a dock.**
   - **3a, client portal (merged):** `MessengerDock` (a round launcher at the bottom right with a red unread count, and a panel in its place; full screen under the header on a phone). It holds Ask Viv (pinned; the old floating panel's logic, kept mounted so an answer in flight survives going back to the list), the primary consultant (an existing conversation with topic "csc", or a place to start one that creates it on the first message), notices from Vivacity and every other conversation. The Inbox and the bubble share `useClientConversationPane`. The launcher icon is `VivMessengerIcon`: the Vivacity logo's white shield, cyan V and purple to fuchsia cape drawn as a speech bubble, with a dark-mode variant. The back-to-top pill moved from the bottom centre to the top centre under the pinned header. Ask Viv still replies only to a question that was sent.
   - **3b, staff portal (in review):** the same dock for Vivacity staff (`StaffMessengerDock`, bottom right). Default view "My clients": conversations of the organisations `get_my_message_scope()` returns, which asks `resolve_notification_recipients` itself, so the bubble and the bell cannot disagree (own clients, clients covered as a delegate such as the all-CSC assistants, watched, and for Super Admins ones with no consultant); a chip says why ("Covering Nova", "Watching", "Unassigned"). An "All clients" switch shows everyone's. Unread is taken from the message notifications routing sent the person, not from participant rows (which fixes delegates and reassigned consultants having no unread state). Reading a conversation that is mine records the read position (and shows the client "Seen") without calling `fn_mark_conversation_read`, which is written for clients. Ask Viv for staff stays its own panel and appears as a thread only when its launcher flag is on; the old staff Ask Viv floating launcher at the bottom right is replaced by the bubble. The separate rollout-flagged Ask Viv assistant widget at the bottom left is unchanged. The migration is applied to production (20 November 2026).
   - **Draggable bubble:** grab the launcher and move it anywhere it is blocking something; it snaps to the nearest edge when let go (left or right, any height between the pinned header and footer), opens its panel on that side, and is remembered on this device (`messenger:dock-position`). It also works by touch, and by keyboard (Shift + arrow keys move it, Shift + Home puts it back); a small press that barely moves is still a click. Phones keep the full-screen panel. Position maths is in `dock/dockPosition.ts`.
   - Still open for phase 3: one realtime listener instead of several, and typing indicators.
4. **Support tickets as threads, and the Help Center retired (in review).** In the client bubble a ticket is a "support" thread: "ST-12 · Title" with its status as the chip ("Received", "In progress", "Waiting for your reply", "Resolved", "Closed"), the replies a client may see (never staff notes, AI drafts or events; the reporter is "You", everyone else "Vivacity team"), attachments, and the ticket's own reply rules (a reply reopens a resolved or closed ticket). A pinned "Contact support" thread starts a ticket: the first message creates it through the same `create_support_ticket` call and AI draft as before, then the bubble moves onto it. A ticket is never counted as unread (the chip says when your reply is wanted). The Help Center drawer (`HelpCenterDrawer`, its chatbot and consultant tabs) is removed; `HelpCenterProvider` and `useHelpCenter` stay as a thin layer so the eleven buttons that called `openHelpCenter("chatbot" | "csc" | "support")` did not need rewriting: they now open the bubble on Ask Viv, the consultant thread or Contact support. `HelpCenterProvider` also supplies the bubble's open state for the client, Academy and staff layouts. The Academy layout now has the bubble (academy-only people get support only, because they cannot use conversations or Ask Viv). **Kept on purpose, to be removed in a later approved cleanup:** the `help-center-chat` edge function and the `help_threads` and `help_messages` tables; older consultant conversations created by the Help Center (topic "csc") are the consultant thread. The full-page support tickets pages are unchanged.
5. **Staff Communications (5a, in review) and the Manage Client Messages tab (5b, not started).** `/communications` is rebuilt on the kit with no tabs. Four compact insight cards sit above two panes: **Needs you** (unread for me, with how long the oldest has waited, highlighted after 24 hours), **Covering** (conversations I cover for a colleague, shown as a count with how many are unread), **Active today**, and **Bulk messages** (scheduled count and when one last went out; it opens the bulk history in a side panel instead of a tab). The first three filter the list; clicking one again clears it. Below are a narrow list (the shared list: search, All / Unread / Type, "My clients" by default through `get_my_message_scope`, "All clients" to browse, a client picker, and for Super Admins a team-member picker that shows the conversations that person is in) and a wide conversation with the shared message box, "Seen", drafts, screenshot paste and a link to the client. The Clients rail (and its "604 threads" header) is gone. The new-message dialog moved unchanged to `components/communications/NewTeamMessageDialog.tsx`; Bulk message keeps its dialog. Insight numbers are computed from the threads on the page (`communicationsInsights`), so they always agree with the list and the bell. Left for later: assigning and resolving a conversation (still on the Manage Client Messages tab, phase 5b), and removing the old `components/messaging` list, thread and rail components once 5b no longer uses them.
6. Schema-backed features: replies, edit and delete, reactions, pin and mute, search.

## Broadcast messages (must not break)

Broadcasts are delivered by `send-broadcast-campaign` and the scheduled-delivery path with the service role: one conversation per organisation (`type = 'broadcast'`), the sender plus every `tenant_users` row as participants, and read tracking in `broadcast_recipients` through `fn_mark_conversation_read`. The messenger treats them as read-only threads under "From Vivacity", and the Inbox source marks them read through that function so `broadcast_recipients.read_at` and the audit entries keep working. Staff Communications, bulk message history and scheduling are not changed by phases 1 to 4.

## Staff Ask Viv inside the bubble

The bottom-left Ask Viv widget (`AskVivAssistantWidget`) and the old top-bar `AskVivButton` are retired. Ask Viv for staff is now the first thread in the staff bubble (`StaffAskVivChat`), shown only where the assistant's own rollout gate (`useAskVivAssistantAccess`) allows it, as the widget was. It uses the same hooks, conversations, usage limit and suggestions; replies only to a question that was sent; and is kept mounted while the bubble is closed so an answer on its way is not lost. Its header has its own full page button (keeps the conversation), so it has no "Open in the Inbox" link. Anything that still calls `useAskVivAssistantWidget().openWidget()` opens the bubble on Ask Viv (bridge in `StaffMessengerDock`). Each portal now has one bubble. Tests: `StaffAskVivChat.test.tsx`, `StaffMessengerDock.test.tsx`. Not yet checked in a signed-in browser.

## Notifications drawer (19 November 2026)

The staff, academy and client bells now open one shared drawer on the right (`NotificationsDrawer`) instead of a popover: grouped by day, one card each, All/Unread switch, one Type dropdown, a tick to mark one read, mark all read, and (staff only) the preferences matrix behind a gear. Grouping and filtering live in `drawerModel.ts` with tests. Data sources and click behaviour are unchanged: staff `useNotifications` / `NotificationDropdown`, client `useClientNotifications` / `ClientTopbar`. Not yet checked in a signed-in browser.

## New message inside the bubble (19 November 2026)

Writing a new message no longer opens a pop-up form. The + button in the bubble (client and staff), and the New message buttons on the client Inbox and on Team Communications, open a compose thread inside the bubble (`ComposePane`, id `compose:new`, hidden from the list). Clients choose an optional subject and category, then write; sending creates the conversation and the bubble moves onto it. Staff choose a client, then everyone at that client or one person (only that person and the sender are participants), then write (`StaffNewMessagePane`, `createStaffConversation`, which keeps the sender-first and row-by-row participant rules). Files are attached after the message is sent and a failure is named without hiding the send. `NewConversationDialog` and `NewTeamMessageDialog` are removed. A message to one person relies on the client Inbox listing conversations the person is a participant in; confirm that live. Not yet checked in a signed-in browser.

## My clients includes conversations I take part in (20 November 2026)

Found when a message sent from the staff portal to Demo RTO (not one of the sender's clients) disappeared from the bubble: "My clients" was only the conversations of clients in `get_my_message_scope()`, so a conversation the sender had started or replied in vanished from their own list. `useStaffMessenger` now adds every conversation the person is a participant in (the same rule the team-member filter uses), returns their ids as `participating`, and treats them as mine for read tracking. Conversations with that client that the person is not in stay out of My clients. Not yet checked in a signed-in browser.
